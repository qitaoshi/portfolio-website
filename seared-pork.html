Seared Pork with Orange Sauce

  • Pork
  • Dinner
  • Sauce
40 minsServes 2

Pork fillet seared golden, sliced and served in a glossy sweet-and-sour orange sauce with sharp red onion.

Ingredients

  • 1 pork fillet, about 400g
  • 2 oranges, juiced, plus a few slices
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 20g butter
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 spring onion, sliced, to serve
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Take the pork out of the fridge 20 minutes before cooking and pat it dry. Season all over with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pan over medium-high heat. Sear the pork on every side until deep golden, about 8 minutes in total.
  3. Turn the heat down to medium and keep cooking, turning now and then, until the centre reaches 63°C, about 8 to 10 minutes more. Rest it on a board for 5 minutes.
  4. In the same pan, soften the garlic for 30 seconds, then add the orange juice, honey and soy sauce. Simmer, scraping up the browned bits, until it thickens enough to coat a spoon.
  5. Take the pan off the heat and swirl in the butter until the sauce turns glossy.
  6. Slice the pork, spoon the sauce onto the plate, lay the slices on top and finish with orange slices, red onion and spring onion.

Notes

Do not move the pork while it sears; it releases from the pan when the crust has formed. The resting juices on the board go straight into the sauce.
