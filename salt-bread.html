Salt Bread (Shio Pan)

  • Bread
  • Japanese
  • Baking
2.5 hrs including proofingMakes 6

Buttery, crisp-crusted Japanese milk bread rolls with a molten salted-butter center.

Ingredients

  • 225g bread flour
  • 55g cake flour (or all-purpose)
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 30g sugar
  • 5g salt
  • 150ml cold milk or water
  • 30g soft unsalted butter, for the dough
  • 6 small blocks cold salted butter, for filling
  • Coarse sea salt, for topping

Method

  1. Mix flours, yeast, sugar and salt, then stir in milk until a shaggy dough forms. Knead in the soft butter until smooth and elastic, about 10 minutes.
  2. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
  3. Divide into 6 pieces. Flatten each, place a block of cold butter at one end, and roll up tightly into a tapered log.
  4. Place seam-side down on a tray, cover, and proof again until puffy, about 40 minutes.
  5. Preheat oven to 230°C (450°F). Mist the rolls with water and sprinkle generously with coarse salt.

Notes

Keep the butter blocks in the freezer until the last second. Cold butter is what creates the hollow, steamy center. A dark metal tray helps the bottoms crisp up.
