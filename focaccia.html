Fresh Focaccia

  • Bread
  • Italian
  • Baking
24 hrs including cold proofServes 8

A tall, airy Italian flatbread with a crisp olive-oil crust and a dimpled, salty top.

Ingredients

  • 500g bread flour
  • 400ml lukewarm water
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 10g salt
  • 60ml olive oil, plus extra for the tin
  • A few sprigs of fresh rosemary
  • Flaky sea salt, for topping

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, yeast and salt in a large bowl, then pour in the water and stir until no dry flour is left. The dough will be wet and sticky; that is right.
  2. Cover and rest for 30 minutes. With wet hands, stretch one side of the dough up and fold it over itself, then turn the bowl and repeat on all four sides. Do this three more times, 30 minutes apart.
  3. Drizzle with a little olive oil, cover tightly and leave in the fridge overnight, up to 24 hours. The slow, cold rise builds flavour and big bubbles.
  4. Pour half the olive oil into a baking tin, tip the dough in and let it warm up and spread for 2 to 3 hours, until puffy and jiggly.
  5. Heat the oven to 230°C (450°F). Drizzle over the rest of the oil, press your fingertips all the way down to dimple the dough, then scatter with rosemary and flaky salt.
  6. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden. Lift out onto a rack straight away so the bottom stays crisp.

Notes

Be generous with the oil in the tin; it fries the base and gives the crisp crust. If the dough springs back when you dimple it, leave it to rest another 30 minutes.
