Chicken Katsu

  • Chicken
  • Japanese
  • Dinner
40 minsServes 2

Crisp panko-crumbed chicken thigh with finely shredded cabbage, rice and tonkatsu sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken thigh fillets
  • 1/2 cup plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 1/2 cups panko breadcrumbs
  • Canola oil, 2cm deep in the pan
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1/4 green cabbage
  • Tonkatsu sauce, to serve
  • 1 1/2 cups medium grain rice

Method

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear. Simmer it with 2 cups of water, covered, for 12 minutes, then leave it off the heat with the lid on for 10 minutes.
  2. Shred the cabbage as finely as you can and drop it into iced water for 5 minutes so it crisps up. Drain well.
  3. Pound the chicken to an even 1cm thickness and season both sides with salt and pepper.
  4. Coat each piece in flour, then egg, then panko. Press the crumbs on firmly so they stay put in the oil.
  5. Heat the oil to 170°C. Fry the chicken for 4 to 5 minutes a side, until deep golden and 74°C in the thickest part.
  6. Rest it on a wire rack for 2 minutes, then slice into strips. Serve with the cabbage, rice and a dish of tonkatsu sauce.

Chef's Notes

Drain the chicken on a rack rather than paper towel; paper traps steam and softens the crust underneath.
