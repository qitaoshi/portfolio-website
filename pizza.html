Pepperoni Pizza

  • Pizza
  • Italian
  • Dinner
Overnight dough, 10 min bakeMakes 2

An overnight dough baked as hot as the oven goes, topped with passata, mozzarella, pepperoni and basil.

Ingredients

  • 300g bread flour
  • 200ml water
  • 3g instant yeast
  • 8g salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150ml passata
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 80g sliced pepperoni
  • A few basil leaves

Method

  1. Stir the flour, water and yeast together and leave for 20 minutes. Add the salt and oil and knead until smooth, about 5 minutes.
  2. Cover and leave in the fridge for 24 hours. In a hurry, 2 hours at room temperature works too.
  3. Split the dough into two balls and let them sit at room temperature until soft and relaxed, 1 to 2 hours.
  4. Heat the oven as hot as it goes, at least 250°C, with a heavy tray or pizza stone inside for 45 minutes.
  5. Stretch each ball by hand to about 28cm, leaving a thick edge. Spread with passata seasoned with a pinch of salt, then add the mozzarella and pepperoni.
  6. Slide onto the hot tray and bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the crust is blistered and the cheese bubbles. Add the basil once it is out.

Chef's Notes

Go easy on the sauce and cheese; too much and the middle stays soggy before the crust is done.
