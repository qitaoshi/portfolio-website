Pork & Cabbage Dumplings

  • Chinese
  • Dumplings
  • Family style
1.5 hrsMakes about 40

Juicy pork and wombok dumplings, pleated by hand and pan-fried until crisp on the bottom.

Ingredients

  • 40 round dumpling wrappers
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1/4 wombok (Chinese cabbage), finely chopped
  • 3 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 thumb of ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Toss the chopped wombok with the salt and leave for 15 minutes. Squeeze out as much water as you can with your hands; this stops the filling going soggy.
  2. Mix the pork mince with the soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, sesame oil and ginger. Stir hard in one direction for a couple of minutes until the mixture turns sticky and paste-like.
  3. Fold in the squeezed wombok and the spring onions until evenly mixed.
  4. Hold a wrapper in your palm, add a heaped teaspoon of filling and wet the edge with water. Fold it in half, then pleat one side against the other to seal. Keep finished dumplings under a damp tea towel.
  5. Heat a little oil in a non-stick pan and set the dumplings in flat-side down. Fry for 2 minutes until golden underneath.
  6. Pour in about 80ml of water and cover straight away. Steam for 6 minutes, then uncover and cook until the water is gone and the bottoms crisp up again.

Notes

Freeze extras on a tray before bagging them so they do not stick together; they cook straight from frozen with an extra 2 minutes of steaming. Serve with soy sauce, black vinegar and chilli oil.
